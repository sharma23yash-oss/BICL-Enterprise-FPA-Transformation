# Limitations

1. Synthetic data and a fictional company; no real client, data, stakeholder input or engagement.
2. Excel-only simulation; tested in LibreOffice 24.2 only. Microsoft Excel verification is pending, including D-09 and D-11.
3. Three defect items cannot be tested in this environment (D-04b, D-08, D-10: data validation, list validation, sheet protection). D-12 (prior forecast and asset roll-forward) is descoped for lack of source data. Sheet protection has no password and is an accidental-edit safeguard, not a security control.
4. No balance sheet, debt schedule, cash-flow statement, headcount plan, fixed-asset register, consolidation or multi-currency. The model is not a three-statement model.
5. No Anaplan build. The mapping, specification and training track are design only.
6. Business case: all costs are placeholders; the Base-case NPV is negative; benefits are hypotheses and none is measured. Possible new CoE roles are not in the TCO.
7. Governance, risk, roadmap and change plans are proposals. No control has operated, no risk is marked Controlled, no readiness survey or training has taken place.
8. Testing was designed and run by the same AI assistant; no independent validation.
9. Phase 1 FY27 figures are historical and unreconciled to v2.1; the difference is unexplained pending source-level reconciliation.
10. Forecast accuracy (K13) and CapEx utilisation (K15) are not calculable with the available data.
11. Not demonstrated by this project: Power BI, SQL, ERP integration, a real Anaplan build, production deployment, client delivery.
